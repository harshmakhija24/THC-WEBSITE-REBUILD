Locked Design System Rules

  • 80/20 Balance: Paper (#F6F3EC) and Ink (#0E1424) carry roughly 80% of any screen.
  • Coral & Teal Fill Rule: Coral (#FF5A4F) and Teal (#0FA3A3) fail contrast as text on Paper (2.8:1). Use them as fills with Ink text (6:1+).
  • Accessible Text Accents: When text or links must be coral or teal on Paper, use --coral-text (#C8372D) and --teal-text (#0B7A7A).
  • Headline Pattern: Plain phrase with ONE italic accent word or phrase in the display serif. Never multiple.
  • No Raw Values: Zero ad-hoc hex values, pixel font sizes, or unapproved shadows in components.
